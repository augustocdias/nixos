# Research brief: <question>

Use this template for substantial research. Replace the prompts with findings and omit sections that do not apply. Keep the ledger in this file; do not create a separate JSON run or claim an automated validator passed.

## Answer and decision

State the answer or recommendation, the decision it supports, constraints and confidence. If the evidence cannot support a decision, identify the missing prerequisite.

## Findings

Present the facts that support the answer with source references. Distinguish verified facts from source claims, weak signals and interpretation. Include relevant versions and distinguish observed behavior from documented behavior.

## Source ledger

For each source, record:

- A source identifier and title.
- The original URL or authorized local path, with relevant section or lines.
- The revision, version or checked date where material.
- The claim it supports and how the evidence was obtained.
- Its lineage, including any copies or shared underlying announcement or dataset.
- Access or extraction limitations that affect the claim.

Explain why corroborating lineages are independent. A single authoritative source can establish the behavior it defines; it cannot alone establish independent adoption or reliability.

## Contrary evidence

Describe the strongest counterexample and its effect on the answer. If none was found, record where and what you searched. If the check does not apply, explain why.

## Gaps and uncertainty

Record unresolved contradictions, stale data, blocked sources and incomplete extraction. Explain which conclusions they weaken and what evidence would change the answer. Do not imply that an unread source was checked.

## Next action

Name the concrete next step and any authorization or evidence it needs. Link supporting artifacts only when they already exist and are accessible to the recipient.
